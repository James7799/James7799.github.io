School life safety & violence recognition

Built a real-time RTSP system for loitering, intrusion, and violence detection. Created a 150-video evaluation set with scene-level separation, compared MViT-v2, Keypoint TCN, and Frame CNN, and fixed false alerts triggered by normal footage around event timelines. 02
Fire & smoke detection

Trained a D-FINE detector on 17,769 images and reached COCO AP@.5:.95 of 0.7985. Reduced difficult false positives such as sunsets and lighting using stationary-object suppression, temporal rules, and CLIP/VLM verification. The system passed KISA certification in June 2026.

03
VLM verification for fall & violence alerts

Integrated Qwen3-VL on Jetson Orin NX to review the six seconds preceding each TCN alert. In a test set, it filtered 25 of 32 false-positive alerts, taking about three seconds per alert. Quantization experiments showed Q4_K_M performed better than Q8_0 for violence recognition in the tested setup.

04
INT8 quantization & edge NPU deployment

Worked across Ambarella CV2S, DEEPX DX-M1, and Jetson TensorRT. Diagnosed a constant INT8 output caused by a test-code dtype mismatch (float32 written into an int16 slot), restoring output correlation to 0.9948. Pose QAT maintained accuracy, with PCK@0.05 improving from 86.94 to 87.14. Reimplemented LSTM behavior with basic operations for NPUs without native LSTM support.

05
Multi-camera real-time fall detection

Developed a pipeline combining person detection, pose estimation, 83-dimensional motion features, and causal TCN. Collected hard negatives from live camera feeds and checked feature/output parity after model changes. The earlier LSTM version reached 97.5–99% accuracy on NTU RGB+D 120.

06
Rainfall intensity estimation from CCTV

Investigated domain leakage after discovering that the initial model learned wet-wall appearance rather than rain streaks. Evaluated camera-specific AdaBN, which reduced clear-day false-positive probability on hospital cameras from 0.91 to 0.05. Results on the Wanju camera remain under investigation.

07
Dataset engineering & leakage checks

Collected 82,426 bag, glasses, and hat crops from approximately 55 hours of footage across three cameras. Built an automatic-labeling and human-review workflow for 1,441 lying-person frames. Found train/validation leakage of 52% and 85% in separate projects and corrected it with group-based splitting.

Edge AI box firmware & production pipeline

Built and released Jetson Orin NX AIBOX firmware v1.0.3–v1.1.0 using Yocto and RAUC. Added VLM verification to an ONVIF Profile M gateway, ported RK3588 + DEEPX tracking/fall/violence pipelines to a C++ daemon (four channels, 9–10 FPS per channel), and implemented LAN mDNS leader election and per-channel RTSP reconnection.
